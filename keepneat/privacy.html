Privacy Policy

KeepNeat: Photo Cleaner · Last updated November 19, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. KeepNeat does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your photos and files

  • KeepNeat asks for access to your photo library to find duplicates, similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots, large videos and media saved by chat apps.
  • All analysis happens on your iPhone. Photos, videos, thumbnails and results are never uploaded anywhere.
  • In the Files screen, KeepNeat only reads folders you choose yourself.
  • Items are deleted only when you tap Delete and confirm in the iOS dialog. Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • Small caches (photo fingerprints, your Bin and swipe progress) stay on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

If KeepNeat offers an in-app purchase, Apple processes the payment through the App Store. We never receive your name, email address or payment details.

Children

KeepNeat is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: support@example.com
