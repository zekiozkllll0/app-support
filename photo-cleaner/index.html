Frequently asked questions

  • I deleted photos but my storage didn't change. iPhone keeps deleted photos in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted → Select → Delete All to free the space.
  • I removed something by mistake. Items you remove first go to the Bin inside the app. Open the Bin and tap Restore. If you already deleted it, it's in Photos → Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • WhatsApp media doesn't show up. The app can only see media saved to your photo library. In WhatsApp, go to Settings → Chats → Save to Photos.
  • Does the app upload my photos? No. Everything happens on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
  • Will it slow my phone down? No. It only looks at small previews and doesn't run in the background.
